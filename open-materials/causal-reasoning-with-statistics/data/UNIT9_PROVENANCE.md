# Unit 9 synthetic-data provenance

`unit9_multisite_replication.csv` is an original synthetic teaching dataset generated by `scripts/generate_unit9.py` with Python `random.Random(20261002)`. No real, copied, reconstructed, pseudonymized, or inferred student record was used.

The file contains 4,000 fictional students at Cedar, Lake, Ridge, and Harbor sites. Each site contains lower- and high-baseline-distress cells. Exactly half of each cell is assigned a brief digital coping program and half comparison resources. The primary binary outcome is clinically meaningful improvement at 12 weeks as assessed by a fictional blinded evaluator. It is unobserved for some students. A complete binary self-report outcome is generated by a separate measurement process.

The recruited composition intentionally differs from a fixed external target composition. All eight target cells are represented in the recruited sample, but reweighting is required to average cell-specific risks over the target. These shares are teaching inputs, not estimates from a real population.

The observation indicator depends only on assignment, site, and baseline distress in the hidden generator. Conditional cell standardization can therefore recover the recruited-sample assignment contrast in repeated simulations, but an analyst could not verify this hidden rule from the observed file. The complete self-report outcome has assignment-dependent sensitivity and specificity relative to the hidden primary outcome. It is a different measurement process, not a gold standard and not a repair for missing blinded assessments.

The generator's cell risks, observation rule, and classification parameters test computational behavior. They are hidden simulator parameters, not evidence available to an analyst and not findings about students, distress, digital programs, follow-up, or self-report. Primary NumPy, independent standard-library Python, and base-R paths reconstruct all displayed estimands and diagnostics.

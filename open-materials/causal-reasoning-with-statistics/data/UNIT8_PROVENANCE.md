# Unit 8 synthetic-data provenance

`unit8_cognitive_training_trial.csv` is an original synthetic teaching dataset generated by `scripts/generate_unit8.py` with Python `random.Random(20260929)`. No real, copied, reconstructed, pseudonymized, or inferred participant record was used.

The file contains 3,200 fictional trial participants. Baseline anxiety is fixed at 1,280 high and 1,920 low values, then shuffled. Within each baseline-anxiety stratum, exactly half of participants are assigned cognitive training and half comparison activities. Baseline anxiety is therefore measured before assignment, prespecified as a moderator, and used as a randomization stratum. Baseline memory is also measured before assignment. Strategy use is measured after assignment and before the follow-up memory outcome.

The generator gives training a constant 7-point effect on strategy use. Its controlled direct outcome effect is `2.5 - 1.5 * baseline_high_anxiety`; strategy use contributes `0.45 * strategy_use_score` to the outcome. The resulting total assignment effect is 5.65 points in the low-anxiety stratum and 4.15 points in the high-anxiety stratum, averaging 5.05 points in the fixed 60/40 cohort composition.

An omitted continuous self-regulation variable raises both strategy use and follow-up memory. This creates deliberate mediator-outcome confounding. Random assignment identifies the total assignment effect overall and within the prespecified baseline-anxiety strata. It does not randomize strategy use. The mediator-conditioned assignment coefficient is retained as a negative teaching example and is not labeled a controlled direct effect or natural direct effect.

The generator-defined total, controlled-direct, and strategy-mediated components test computational recovery. They are hidden simulator parameters, not analyst evidence and not findings about real cognitive training, anxiety, strategy use, or memory. Primary NumPy, standard-library Python, and base-R paths independently reconstruct all displayed estimates.

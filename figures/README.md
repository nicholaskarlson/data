# Essential Explanatory Figures

These six figures are seeded and source-generated from the published synthetic datasets or a fixed-seed resampling procedure. They are explanatory book figures, not captured software interfaces. Their committed PNGs are checksum-pinned release assets; equivalent re-renderings can differ at the byte level across Python, numeric-library, font, and rasterizer environments.

- `fig08_resampling_means.png`: fixed-seed bootstrap distributions at two resample sizes.
- `fig12_belonging_exam.png`: Study 07 focal scatterplot and fitted line.
- `fig20_factorial_interaction.png`: Study 05 verified cell-mean pattern.
- `fig21_mood_profile.png`: Study 06 group-by-week means.
- `fig25_habit_series.png`: Study 11 chronological habit counts.
- `fig25_stress_series.png`: Study 11 chronological ordinal stress ratings.

Each PNG is at least 1,500 × 900 pixels and carries approximately 300 dpi metadata. `SHA256SUMS` records the exact release bytes.
